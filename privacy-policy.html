Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

K8s Cost Ops ("we", "us") operates k8scostops.com. This page explains how we handle information collected through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit the audit request form, we collect your name, work email address, company name, and any details you provide about your infrastructure. We use this information solely to prepare your audit and follow up with you directly. It is never sold or shared for marketing purposes.

Cookies and visitor identification

When you visit our website, cookies and similar technologies may be used by our online data partners or vendors to associate these activities with other personal information they or others have about you, including by association with your email. We (or service providers on our behalf) may then send communications and marketing to these email addresses. You may opt out of receiving this advertising by visiting https://app.retention.com/optout.

You also have the option to opt out of the collection of your personal data in compliance with GDPR by visiting https://www.rb2b.com/rb2b-gdpr-opt-out.

Third-party services

This site uses RB2B for visitor identification. Analytics and scheduling are handled by third-party services (Google Analytics, Calendly). These services have their own privacy policies.

Data retention

Form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to follow up on your audit request. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hubert@k8scostops.com.
